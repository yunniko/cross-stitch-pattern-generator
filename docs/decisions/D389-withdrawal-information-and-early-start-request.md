# D389 · Withdrawal information and the early-start request are separate documents, both sent in full
Date: 2026-10-09 · Goal: G-129 M4 · Status: active (superseded by: —)
Context: the one "withdrawal" text a buyer ticked said the right was lost, which D387's proportionate refund contradicts, and a link is not a durable copy.
Decision: "withdrawal" becomes the full information (public at `/withdrawal`), a new "early-start" document is the request ticked before paying; a consent records both with the terms; the purchase and upgrade mails repeat the request and carry the terms and withdrawal information in full; a withdrawal is acknowledged by mail when it is recorded.
Force: judgment — Directive 2011/83 Art. 8(7) and 14(3) as read for the private legal draft; the Owner's lawyer may adjust it.
Rejected: keeping one combined text (the information would be agreed to, not given); links only in the mail (not a durable medium, CJEU C-49/11).
Consequence: buying needs all four documents published; the Owner publishes them from the private draft before launch.
Evidence: tests/unit/billing-consent.spec.ts; tests/e2e/billing-change.spec.ts; tests/e2e/billing-withdraw.spec.ts
