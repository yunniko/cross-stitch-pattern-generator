### G-115 · Fill knows stitch types; the brush changes a stitch's type; thinner half stitches; the Zoom tool's direction — DONE (2026-10-06, deployed 2026-10-06, Owner sign-off 2026-10-07)
- **What:** (1) Fill takes the stitch type into account: a region is the touching stitches of the same colour **and** the same type, so a press on whole stitches does not spread into half stitches of that colour. Two new Fill switches in its options: **Color only**, which changes only the colour, keeps each stitch's type, and ignores type when finding the region; and **Diagonal neighbours**, on: stitches touching at a corner count (as now), off: only up, down, left and right. The brush's double-press fill and its option go. (2) The brush with no colour chosen changes the type of a stitch it passes over to the chosen stitch type and keeps its colour; on an empty stitch it does nothing. (3) Half stitches drawn thinner: the cut corners go from 50 % to 60 % of the side. (4) The Zoom tool gets a choice, Zoom in or Zoom out, for a left press; a right press does the other.
- **Why:** the Owner's live review, 2026-10-06: fill spread across stitch types, the double press got in the way, half stitches read too heavy, and zooming out needed Shift.
- **Acceptance criteria:** each behaviour above works in the Edit workspace with symmetry and the transparency lock as the tools already honour them, and is undoable as one step; the two Fill switches and the Zoom choice are kept with the other tool options across a reload; the double-press fill and its option are gone from the app and the brief; half stitches are drawn with 60 % corners in every view and every export (PNG, PDF, A4, the Stitched view, the brush outline, the stitch-type buttons), with the browser and the Rust exporter agreeing; unit tests for the region rules and e2e tests for each behaviour; the brief, release notes and decisions updated; deployed and checked live.
- **Constraints:** the Rust exporter's half-stitch constant must match the browser's (D259), compelled by the byte-identity harness; admin pages are never exercised live (Owner).
