# Exploratory QA, 2026-10-04, second pass (G-091 M3)

**Mode and scope:** scoped pass on what G-091 changed: opening, starting and generating charts, and the state carried across
them. Local production build with the Rust engine, uncommitted G-091 M2/M3 work on top of commit 84e25c9. A throwaway
Playwright probe drove the interface and printed what it saw; nothing here rests on reading code alone.

## Findings

| # | Severity | Finding | Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | wrong | With Crop in hand and `5` typed in Left, opening another chart reset the frame but the field still showed `5` (the typed text outlived the number it stood for) | **Fixed**: the number wins over typed text once the frame changes from outside; e2e added |
| 2 | stuck (found before the probe, while wiring) | A new chart arriving with Crop in hand closed the frame but left Crop in hand with nothing to show, the same shape as finding 2 of the first pass | **Fixed**: the tool stays in hand with a fresh frame over the new chart; same e2e |
| 3 | note | A new chart opened while the Stitched view is in force stays in the Stitched view, where nothing can be edited until another view is chosen | Left; recorded in D283 and the design brief as the behaviour. The Owner may prefer a new chart to open in the Color view |
| 4 | note | "Keep editing" in the new-chart confirmation returns to the start screen, not to the chart; "Back to your chart" is a second step | Left: as designed (Atelier), but two steps to undo one press |

## What held

A piece in hand is gone after opening a file and nothing can be applied; zoom returns to 100 %; undo starts empty; the tool in
hand is kept; the Threads settings are shown. A file with missing dimensions, an OXS file with no chart size and an empty
file are each refused with their message and leave the open chart untouched. A reload restores the chart with a clean
history. An empty grid created over a chart is what a reload restores. No page error occurred; the three console errors
were the app's own logged reports of the three refused files.

## Coverage

**Exercised:** opening over a crop frame, over a piece in hand and in the Stitched view; the confirmation and its way back;
three bad files; reload after generating and after creating an empty grid.

**Not exercised:** cancelling a generation and regenerating (the probe's third scenario timed out before reaching them; the
existing suite covers both); the bundle (`.cspzip`) and editable-file paths; restore of a damaged autosave; the live site;
anything outside G-091's scope.
