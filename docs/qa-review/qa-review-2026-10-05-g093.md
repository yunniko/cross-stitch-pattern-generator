# Exploratory QA, 2026-10-05 (G-093)

**Mode and scope:** scoped pass on what G-093 changed: the options of the tool in hand (M1), the keyboard shortcuts now driven
by the command table (M2), and the command list (M3). Local production build with the Rust engine, on the uncommitted G-093
work. A throwaway Playwright probe drove the interface, printed what it saw and took screenshots that were looked at. M1 and
M2 intend no change of behaviour, so for them the question was "is anything different", and the automated suite is the larger
part of the answer.

## What held

**Keys.** F chooses Fill; Ctrl+B does nothing; Shift+L chooses Line. Space held borrows Pan and gives the tool back. Ctrl+Z
undoes; Ctrl+Shift+Z and Ctrl+Shift+Y both redo, as before. Typing "b3x" in a text entry changes no tool, view or colour.
After a command is run from the list a chart key acts at once.

**The list.** 75 commands, all shown, grouped. Searches with regular-expression characters, a backslash, markup, 500
characters or letters no command has return nothing or the literal matches, and never fail; leading and trailing spaces and
capitals are ignored; "ctrl" finds the two commands with that key and "esc" the six on Escape. Enter with nothing matching
leaves the list open. Up from the first usable command wraps to the last. Tab leaves the focus in the search. A press
outside the list closes it; Escape closes it and returns the focus to its control.

**Commands on what is in hand.** With a piece in hand the eleven selection commands are usable except Paste (nothing copied)
and Fill (no colour in hand), each with its reason; Undo is not usable and says why. Copy then makes Paste usable. "Crop the
chart to the piece" cropped 50 × 31 to 7 × 5 and Ctrl+Z restored it. "Open a pattern file" with a chart open asks before
replacing it and opens no file chooser when the answer is to keep editing. "Export the editable file" downloaded
`sample_editable.json`. "Generate or regenerate the chart" regenerated. "New chart" opened the start screen, where the list's
control is unavailable.

No page or console error occurred.

## Coverage

**Exercised:** the list's search, keyboard, pointer and closing; one or more commands from File, Generate, Edit, Tools,
Selection, Chart and View run from the list; the key edges above; a 390 × 700 and a 1280 × 600 page.

**Not exercised by the probe:** the Backstitch and Crop commands from the list (their keys are covered by the suite), the
Colours commands, the photo commands with no chart, a Mac keyboard (Cmd is matched by the same code as Ctrl; the list always
writes "Ctrl"), touch, a screen reader. The tool options of M1 were probed during M1 (the proof in the goal log) and not
again here. The full suite, 554 cases, passed on the final code. The live site is checked after deploy.
