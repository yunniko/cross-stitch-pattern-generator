# QA review — G-104 (a colour picker held on a key), 2026-10-06

**Mode and scope:** changed, commits `8c4d6b2..d3dcb2f`. Scoped to G-104's surfaces: the Picker tool (key I, both
buttons, a backstitch line, an empty cell), Alt held from the six tools that paint, Alt with focus on a rail button and
in a text entry, a tool chosen while Alt is down, the window losing focus mid-hold, Space and Alt together, the command
list, the rail and the phone width. Run against a local production build (`next start`, port 30200), driven by a
temporary Playwright script, since deleted. Admin pages were not touched.

## What was exercised

- **Alt with a rail button focused.** The Picker came into hand. A press took the stitch, and Fill came back on release.
- **Alt in the pattern name field.** Nothing was borrowed; Fill stayed in hand.
- **A tool chosen while Alt is down.** Brush, chosen mid-hold, stayed after Alt came up (D319).
- **Losing focus while Alt is down.** A window blur gave Line back at once; the late keyup changed nothing.
- **Space and Alt together.** Whichever comes first is the one borrowed. The other does nothing until it is released, and
  the original tool returns after both (Oval in both orders).
- **Undo.** A pick with Alt was no undo step: Undo stayed disabled.
- **The browser's Alt menu.** The keyup after a borrow was cancelled. A plain Alt tap with Select in hand was left to the
  browser.
- **A right press on a backstitch line.** It took the line's thread into the background.
- **Command list.** "pick" finds "Pick a color while the key is held" (Alt (held), keyboard only) and "Picker tool" (I).
- **Cursor.** The chart shows the dropper cursor with the Picker in hand.
- **Phone width.** At 375 px the Picker is in the rail (`shots/g104-phone.png`).
- **Page errors.** None were raised.
- **Browser runs.** `color-picker` 5 of 5 against the rebuilt build after the fix.

## Coverage

Covered: every input that reaches the picker (I, Alt held, both buttons, the rail, the command list), focus states,
sequencing with other held keys and tool changes, Stitched view, and the phone width.

Not covered:
- **A real Alt+Tab.** Only a synthetic blur event was sent; headless Chromium cannot switch programs.
- **The browser's own Alt menu.** It was judged by `defaultPrevented`, not seen.
- **macOS Option.** No Mac was available; only the label is unit-tested.
- **The live site.** It is checked after the deploy.
