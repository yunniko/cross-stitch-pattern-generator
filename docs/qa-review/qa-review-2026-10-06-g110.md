# QA review — G-110 (the view as switches), 2026-10-06

**Mode and scope:** changed, commits `f3732006..8c4d6b2` (77 files) plus the uncommitted fix below. Scoped to G-110's
surfaces: the pattern mode, Symbols, Photo, the pattern visibility slider, keys 1–5, Y and P, the view-only note, a
reload keeping the view, a new chart resetting it, and the photo-slider fix. Run against a local production build
(`next start`, port 30200), driven by a temporary Playwright script, since deleted. Admin pages were not touched.

## Findings

| # | Severity | Finding | Repro | Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | usability | **With the visibility slider focused, every view key did nothing.** After the slider is moved, 4, 5, Y, P and 1–3 were swallowed until the focus left it. The shortcut hook treated every `input` as a text field, so a focused checkbox swallowed every shortcut too | Photo on, Tab to the slider, press 5: the view stayed "Pattern, Color, 100% over the photo" | **Fixed:** `use-keyboard-shortcuts.ts` treats an input that takes no typed characters (range, checkbox, radio, …) like a button. Text fields keep their keys. Space still acts only on the body or the canvas, and arrows and Enter go to the slider through `use-keyboard-cursor.ts`. New case in `tests/e2e/view-switches.spec.ts` (with a check that typing "p4" into the name field changes nothing) |
| 2 | note (pre-existing) | At 375 px the page scrolls 109 px sideways with the photo shown alone | `shots/g110-phone-photo-alone.png` | Not G-110's: the phone layout is G-101. The view bar wraps and its switches stay reachable |
| 3 | note | Three console errors "503" during the run | the whole script | Not investigated; their source is unknown. No page error (uncaught exception) was raised |

## What was exercised

- **The slider by keyboard.** Home goes to 0 %. Each arrow adds 1 %: the note "Too faint to edit…" shows at 4 % and is
  gone at 5 %. End goes to 100 %.
- **A press on the chart at a faint visibility.** It changed nothing: Undo stayed disabled.
- **Stitched.** Symbols and Photo are disabled, each with its reason in the title. The choice comes back in Color
  (`shots/g110-bar-stitched.png`).
- **Other workspaces.** The note shows only in Edit.
- **Reload and new charts.** A reload keeps the view. Opening `sample.oxs` resets it to Color with no photo. A chart
  without a photo has Photo disabled ("No source photo…", `shots/g110-bar-no-photo.png`), and keys P and 5 do nothing.
- **Half and alone.** The pattern half over the photo, and the photo alone (`shots/g110-half-over-photo.png`,
  `shots/g110-photo-alone.png`).
- **The photo-slider fix.** Saturation moved to −100 and straight back to 0 left `data-photo="uploaded"`.
- **Browser runs.** `view-switches` (4) and `keyboard-shortcuts` (8): 12 of 12 against the rebuilt local build.

## Coverage

**Not exercised:**
- Interactive Chrome: the Chrome session froze on screenshots, so the script replaced it.
- Pointer drag on the slider: only keyboard and `fill` were used.
- Touch.
- Contrast: judged by eye from the screenshots, not measured.
- The full e2e suite: it runs in CI because local memory is short.
