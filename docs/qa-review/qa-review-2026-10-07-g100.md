# QA review — G-100 (dither patterns declared once; built previews; the drawn marks' preview from the server), 2026-10-07

**Mode and scope:** changed, commits `cb42113..88966ca` as they touch dithering: the chooser and its built pictures, the line screens' direction, the photo pane's settings control, the larger preview (built picture or server), `/api/dither-previews` and its limit. The rest of the diff (docs, Rust internals, CI) is covered by the suites, not this pass. Local production build on port 30200 with the processor on 8102 running the release `cs-job`; never the live site.

## What was exercised
- **Suites on that build:** `dithering.spec.ts` and `features.spec.ts`, 15 of 15.
- **`/api/dither-previews` by hand (curl).**
  - Valid requests: Hand-drawn at 56 × 56, 300 × 200, 1000 × 700 and 1500 × 1500 with a full texture took 37, 29, 106 and 261 ms, including the app's hop. Bayer 4×4 and Off also drew.
  - Refused by name, 400: an unknown pattern (the message lists all 13); width 0, −5, 1e9, 56.5 and "56"; a missing height; an out-of-range or non-object texture; an extra key; invalid JSON; an array; an empty body.
  - Also refused: no Origin (403), GET (405), a 20 KB body (413).
  - Limit (90 a minute): 150 parallel requests gave 34 × 200 and 116 × 429 with `retry-after: 1`. 95 sequential requests all passed, since the bucket refills 1.5 a second.
- **The UI in Chrome** (by DOM script once the window was hidden; see Coverage).
  - The chooser shows ten pictures in the declared order, every one a `/dither-previews/*-tile.png` loaded at 24 px.
  - Hand-drawn: one server request, a 44 ms round trip.
  - A ten-step drag on Mark spacing at 40 ms a step sent one request, and the picture changed.
  - Clicking the preview reshuffles the marks: one request.
  - Lines shows four directions, and choosing "|" keeps Lines checked and shows `lines-vertical.png` with no server request. Bayer 4×4 shows its built picture with no request, and the directions are gone. Off shows no preview.
  - A refused preview (429, injected once) shows the server's message under the preview, keeps the last picture, and the next rest draws again and clears the message.
  - Console: no errors, through the pass and on reload.

## Findings
- **Cosmetic, not fixed:** a partial texture (`{"seed":7}`) and an out-of-range one both get "ditherTexture is not a valid texture." with no field named. This is the same validator generation uses, and the app always sends a full texture, so only a hand-made request sees it.
- **Cosmetic, not fixed:** an array body is refused as "0 is not part of a dither preview.", which is accurate but odd; it is refused either way.
- Nothing broken, and no output differed from the design brief.

## Coverage

- **Not exercised here:**
