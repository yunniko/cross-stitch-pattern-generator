# QA review — G-116 (selection modes, Invert selection, the Magic wand), 2026-10-07

**Mode and scope:** changed, commits `88966ca..dc0930b` as they touch selection: the three selection modes, Invert selection, the Magic wand (its region switches, its backstitch click), the piece outline, and the selection bar. Local production build on port 30200 with the processor on 8102; never the live site, no admin pages. The Chrome window was hidden (screenshots time out), so the exploratory cases ran as a Playwright script against the same build; the script stayed in the session scratchpad.

## What was exercised
- **Suites on that build:** magic-wand, selection-modes, fill-rules, lasso-select, selection-actions, selection-keys, command-list and features, 53 of 53. The `@alone` admin-features spec gave 5 of 5 when run by itself.
- **The wand's region against an independent flood** of the clicked stitch's colour and type on the sample chart (50 × 31, 16 colours). At three points under both Diagonal and Edges only, the wand, then Fill selection with Empty, then Enter, emptied exactly the flood's cells: 138, 138 and 104, 6 of 6.
- **Modes:** Select − of the area just selected leaves nothing selected. The mode chosen under the wand is the one Lasso shows.
- **Invert:** with nothing selected it selects the whole chart. On a 300 × 300 grid, inverting a whole-chart selection leaves nothing selected (180 ms). Undo restores the chart.
- **Speed:** a wand click to its selection took 20 ms on the sample and 54 ms on a 300 × 300 empty grid (90,000 stitches).
- **Keys:** a W typed into the Commands search leaves the Brush in hand.
- **Console:** no errors in any case.

## Findings
- **Usability, not fixed; needs an Owner decision.** With the Magic wand in hand, every selection action (Invert, Copy, … Apply here, Cancel) is out of sight at 1440 px and 1280 px. The tool track is 924 px wide at 1440, and the wand's bar needs 1574 px: the Region switches add 395 px to Select's 1123. The track's scrollbar is hidden, so only Shift+wheel or a touchpad reaches them. Enter, Escape, the copy keys and the command list still work. They show from 1680 px. Select's bar at 1440 already lost about 200 px, with middle actions under the pinned Apply and Cancel, and the Brush's lock is out of sight at 1440 too.
  - Tried and withdrawn: letting the track wrap onto a second row showed everything at every width. But the bar's height then changed with the tool, moving the chart under the pointer, and two color-picker cases failed (6 of 6 without it).
  - Evidence: `shots/g116-bar-1440.png`, `shots/g116-bar-1024.png`.
  - **Resolved by the Owner (D333):** the actions moved to a Selection tab in the panel, in six groups, with Apply here and Cancel on the bar as well. Re-measured on a fresh production build at 1024, 1280, 1440 and 1920 px: for Select, Lasso and the wand, all 12 actions are in the tab and the pair is on the bar (`shots/g116-selection-tab-1440.png`, `-1024.png`, `-1920.png`).
  - **Still open:** a tool's own options overflow. At 1440 px the wand's Region switches run under the pinned pair ("Color only" is hidden) and the Brush's lock is out of sight. At 1280 px the Brush also loses its four symmetry switches, and at 1024 px most of its options. The Brush's overflow predates G-116.
- **Cosmetic, fixed with D333:** the Crop to selection title said "this rectangle"; with a wand area it crops to the area's box. It now says "the box around the selection".
- **Not a defect:** Delete does nothing to a selection. It deletes only in Backstitch edit (design brief, keyboard table), as before G-116.
- Nothing broken, and no output differed from the design brief.
