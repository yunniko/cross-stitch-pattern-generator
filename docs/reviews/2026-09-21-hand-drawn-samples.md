# G-054 M3 · The hand-drawn sample sheet

Written 2026-09-21. The sheet itself is regenerated by
`HAND_DRAWN_SAMPLES_OUT=<dir> npm run samples:hand-drawn`, which writes 24 PNGs and commits none of them: every
image is the same chart twice — undithered on the left, `hand-drawn` on the right — so the marks are the only
difference on the page.

## What is in it

Four fixtures (a shaded form, the project's photo-like fixture, a three-channel gradient, four flat regions), at 80,
200 and 600 stitches, on Full range at 20 colours and DMC at 24. The fixtures are generated rather than photographed,
which is deliberate: they are the same sources the numbers in `2026-09-21-dithering-comparison.md` come from, and a
real photo would make the sheet prettier without making the comparison fairer. The shaded form is the one worth
looking at first — smooth shading over a rounded shape is where an undithered chart bands and where a drawn mark has
the most to do.

## Seeing the marks on their own

A chart mixes the four shapes, so no chart shows any one of them clearly:

    HAND_DRAWN_SHAPES_OUT=<dir> npm run shapes:hand-drawn

writes one sheet per shape — ring, broken ring, dot, lump, and the shipped mix — each drawn at 12%, 25%, 40%, 60% and
80% tone on the same placement, with every mark forced to that shape. It composes the exported pieces the pipeline
itself uses (`markLibrary` plus `shapeScore`), so what it draws is what generation draws; nothing about it is a
separate rendering path.

## What it cost, measured

| | Value | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Median error × the undithered chart | 0.78 | `2026-09-21-dithering-comparison.md` |
| Median added confetti | +3.8 pts | same, and the cheapest three of the ten patterns |
| Local tone over a 5×5 | no worse than undithered on all three fixtures | `tests/unit/dither-hand-drawn.spec.ts` |
| Tone held on a flat patch | within 0.02 at five tones | same |
| Generation at 1500 stitches | 5.6 s, against 11.9 s undithered | measured in this session |

The last row is not a typo: a dithered chart skips every smoothing pass (D199), and those passes cost more than
drawing the marks does.

## What is still open

Whether it reads as hand-drawn is the Owner's call, and M4 does not start until that answer exists (G-054 criterion
6). Two things a reader of the sheet should know:

- **The marks are visible at 80 stitches and subtle at 600.** They are sized in stitches, so a bigger chart carries
  more of them rather than bigger ones — the same decision that keeps a 600-stitch chart from looking screened.
- **On flat regions the marks become a grain** rather than a pattern: with nothing to shade, a mark has no room to
  grow and the chart reads as texture. That is visible in the flat-regions and photo-like sheets and is inherent to
  dithering a flat area, not specific to this family.
