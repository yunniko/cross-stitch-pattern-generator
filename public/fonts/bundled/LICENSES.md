# Bundled fonts

Every font here is under a licence that allows it to be bundled and served with a product, and none is modified or renamed.
The licence text or note sits beside each font. Fonts were taken on 2026-10-01 from the three sources below, and are served from
this site only: no page asks Google or anyone else for them. The review of every font considered, and why some were left out,
is `docs/reviews/2026-10-01-bundled-font-licences.md`.

Licences used: **SIL Open Font License 1.1** (https://openfontlicense.org), **CC0-1.0** (a public-domain dedication,
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) and **Public domain (publisher's statement)**.
