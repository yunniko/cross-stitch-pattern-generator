The admin area has a new look. **Overview** shows generations, exports, new and active accounts for today, 7 days, 30 days or all time, compared with the period before, with jobs per day and exports by kind. **Users** can be searched and filtered by role and status, and opens a person's details beside the list. A new **Change log** lists every change an admin made, including roles and logins.
