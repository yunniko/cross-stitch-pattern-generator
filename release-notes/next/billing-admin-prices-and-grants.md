The admin area has a new Billing page for making tiers and their prices. A new price for a tier replaces the old one for new buyers, and the people already on the old price keep it. From a person's page under Users, the admin can also give someone a tier until a chosen day, or end it early; an admin cannot give a tier to their own account. The person then sees on their Plan page that the site gave them the plan and until when. Paid plans are not on sale yet, so only a tier given this way reaches anyone.
