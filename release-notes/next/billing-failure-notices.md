When a renewal payment fails, the person is now told by mail and on their Plan page: until when the plan is kept, when the card is tried again, and a link to pay, with one more reminder three days before the plan would end, a note when the account moves to the free plan with every chart kept, and a note once the payment goes through. Each message goes once per failure. The admin sets the grace on a new Settings page. Paid plans are not on sale yet, so nobody sees this.
