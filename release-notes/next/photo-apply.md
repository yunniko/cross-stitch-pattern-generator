The photo sliders now preview a change until you press **Apply**, which writes it into the photo and sets the sliders back to the middle; **Cancel**, another tab or another workspace gives the change up. With a selection from the Photo wand, Apply changes only the selected part. Apply and background removal can both be undone, and Generate always uses the photo as applied; a note by Generate says when the sliders have moved but are not applied.
