## Changed

- Your account has a new look: a header with a way back to the editor, a list of its sections at the side, and a Profile & sign-in page showing your name, how you sign in, your password and deleting the account.
- Your account has three new sections. **Plan** shows the plan you are on. **Usage** shows how many patterns you generated and exported in the last 30 days and in all, with a bar for each day and your exports by kind. **Preferences** lets you change the editor's preferences without opening a chart.
- The admin area has a new look. **Overview** shows generations, exports, new and active accounts for today, 7 days, 30 days or all time, compared with the period before, with jobs per day and exports by kind. **Users** can be searched and filtered by role and status, and opens a person's details beside the list. A new **Change log** lists every change an admin made, including roles and logins.
