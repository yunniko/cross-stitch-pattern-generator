## New

- Your account has a **Charts** section: the charts you saved, with the space they use. Each one can be opened in the editor, renamed or deleted; a chart opened from there saves back to itself.
- Save is now a menu. Signed in, **Save** keeps the chart in your account, and saving again writes over that same chart even after a rename; **Save as copy** keeps a new one and carries on with it. If the chart was saved from somewhere else since, you are asked first. **Save to file** downloads the editable file as before.
