## New

- Add stamp: a new button beside Save opens a gallery of your stamps, to search and choose one. It arrives on the chart as a piece in hand, to move, flip and apply; a thread the chart lacks is added to its palette.
- Save as stamp: with a piece in hand, the Selection tab can keep it with your account as a named stamp: its stitches, shape, backstitch and the threads it uses. Your stamps are listed in the account under Stamps, to search, rename, pin and delete.

## Changed

- Select, Lasso and the Magic wand have a new **Transparency as colour** switch. Off (the default), the empty stitches of a piece you move, flip or turn leave the stitches beneath them as they were; on, they clear what they land on, as before.
