## New

- A selection can now be cut: Cut (or Ctrl+X) takes the piece off the chart and keeps it to paste.

## Changed

- Paste puts the piece onto the layer you are working on. A cut piece, or a copy pasted onto another layer, arrives in the place it was taken from.
- On a chart with more than one layer, the selection actions now name the layer a selection takes from, so selecting over stitches on another layer no longer seems to do nothing.

## Fixed

- Crop to selection works on a chart with more than one layer, cropping every layer.
