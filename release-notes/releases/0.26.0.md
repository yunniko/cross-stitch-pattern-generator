## New

- Keep thread systems of your own: upload a list of threads as a CSV (number, name, colour) or JSON file, from your account's Thread systems or under the palette choice, and generate, set up palettes, add colours and edit in it. They are yours alone; rename, download or delete them in your account.

## Changed

- Anchor charts now match each colour straight against Anchor's own threads, so a few colours may come out differently from before. Anchor's note still says its colours come from their DMC equivalents.
- A colour whose thread belongs to a system this site does not have, such as one from another app's file, now keeps its system, number and name everywhere: opening, editing, saving, and every export. Its colour editor opens in the plain colour picker.
