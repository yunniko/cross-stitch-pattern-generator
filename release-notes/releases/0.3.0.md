## New

- A site's administrator can switch off the Photo, Edit or Export workspace. A workspace switched off is either gone or shown greyed with a note saying it is not available to you, and so is everything under it: its tab, its commands and keys, and buttons such as Save or "Continue in Edit". You land in the next workspace that is on, and the chart you had open stays as it was.

## Fixed

- The start screen said "Drop a photo anywhere below", but a dropped photo was not taken, and the browser could open it in place of the editor. A photo dropped there now starts a chart from it, as choosing one does. When Photo is not available to you the hint is not shown and a dropped file is ignored. The photo card is no longer highlighted while it is greyed out.
