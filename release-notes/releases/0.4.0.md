## Changed

- The view is now a set of switches under the chart: Color, Black & white or Stitched; **Symbols** on or off (Y); and **Photo** under the pattern (P), with a slider for how visible the pattern is over it, down to the photo alone. Color over the photo can be edited like Black & white. Keys 4 and 5 still show the pattern half over the photo and the photo alone. The view you chose is kept when the page is reloaded; a new chart still opens in Color.

## Fixed

- Setting the photo sliders back to the centre while the photo is shown under the chart now shows the photo as uploaded. A sharpened picture of the old slider values could arrive a moment later and stay on screen.
- Keyboard shortcuts now work while a slider or a checkbox has the focus, for example right after moving the pattern visibility slider. Text fields still keep every key you type.
