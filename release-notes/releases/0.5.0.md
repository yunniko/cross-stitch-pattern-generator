## New

- A colour picker, the Picker (I): click a stitch or a backstitch line to take its colour, or right-click to take it as the second colour. With a drawing tool in hand, hold Alt to pick without changing tools.

## Fixed

- Holding Space to pan no longer leaves the Pan tool in hand if you switch to another window before letting go.
