## New

- The status bar now names the colour under the pointer, with its swatch, beside the stitch numbers. Over a backstitch line it names the line's thread; over an empty stitch it says "Empty (no stitch)".

## Changed

- With the pattern faded below 5 % over the photo, the note "Too faint to edit" is no longer shown. Drawing still waits until the pattern is at least 5 % visible. The pattern visibility slider stays in its place, disabled, while the photo is off, instead of disappearing.
