## New

- With no thread chosen, the Brush changes the stitch type of the stitches it passes over and keeps their colour: choose a stitch type, press the active thread again to let it go, and brush. Empty stitches are left alone.
- The Zoom tool has a new option under **Direction**: **In** or **Out**. A right click zooms the other way, and the pointer shows which way a click goes.

## Changed

- Fill now keeps to the stitch type it is pressed on: filling whole stitches no longer spreads into half stitches of the same colour. Two new Fill options, under **Region**: **Color only** changes just the colour, keeps each stitch's type and fills across types; **Diagonal neighbours** can be set to **Edges only**, so stitches touching only at a corner are left out. The Brush's double press no longer fills a region, and its preference is gone.
- Half stitches are drawn thinner: each cut corner now takes 60 % of the side instead of 50 %, on screen and in the chart images and pages.
